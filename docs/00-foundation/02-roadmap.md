# World Fabric Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Perfect Canonical Record
Design and validate the corridor record using the Middle Corridor.

## Phase 2 — Validation Protocol
Prove that records can be challenged and publication can be blocked for defensible reasons.

## Phase 3 — Production Pipeline
Make research, normalisation, drafting, review, publication and monitoring reproducible.

## Phase 4 — Industrialization
Automate consistency and generation without automating away scientific judgment.

## Phase 5 — Global Expansion
Expand through the global coverage matrix after three methodological stress tests.

## Phase 6 — Intervention Layer
Say which works would relieve documented bottlenecks, on documented evidence.
Gated on Phase 1 and 2: an intervention requires a validated corridor record and
its bottleneck register. Candidates may be authored earlier, marked as such.

### Stress-test order
1. Middle Corridor — multimodal terrestrial
2. Suez / Red Sea — maritime chokepoint system
3. Europe–Asia cables — digital network
