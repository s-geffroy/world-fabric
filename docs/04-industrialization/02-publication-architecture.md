## Home-page doctrine

The home page should answer: **What is changing in the material architecture of globalization?**

It should feature validated transformations, current research coverage, a map entry point and transparent research status. Counts of projects/assets are secondary.

## Public record doctrine

A corridor page is generated from the canonical record. React components may present data interactively, but no independently authored component may contain a divergent analytical conclusion.
